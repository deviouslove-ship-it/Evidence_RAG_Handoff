# Amendments and effective snapshots

An amendment (sửa đổi) relation is reviewed as a change of its own on the node. The CURATOR creates the draft. A REVIEWER approves before the edge joins a snapshot. A document that amends (sửa đổi) one Clause (Khoản) does not remove effectivity (hiệu lực) from the other Clauses.

A registered snapshot is staged and then published atomically. Each run has a `snapshot_revision`. From the moment evidence is pinned, every source in the run must use the same snapshot. If the snapshot is stale or the graph is indeterminate, the system returns temporal unknown and blocks a legal conclusion.

## Update according to the current source (`backend/app/effectivity/`)

- A relation in `node_amendments` (migration `0011_effectivity.sql`) may be at administrative-document (văn bản) scope (`scope = 'document'`) or at provision (điều khoản) scope (`scope = 'node'`). It is generated by the rule reader (`extract.py`), by an AI proposal at the `relation` stage (`ai.py`), or by the creator.
- Only a `REVIEWER` who is not the proposer and who has `can_review` on the catalog of the source or target administrative document (văn bản) may approve (`service.py`). For a relation at `node` scope, the system requires a `target_node_id` to be attached before approval. If it is missing, the relation is rejected with the code `RELATION_TARGET_REQUIRED`.
- Each source approval, relation approval, or confirmation or revocation of effectivity (hiệu lực) automatically publishes a new snapshot (the 3 most recent copies are kept) and writes a hash-chain audit log together with `superseded_by`.
