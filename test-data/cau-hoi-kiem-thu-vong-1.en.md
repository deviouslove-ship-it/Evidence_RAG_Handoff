# Round-1 set of 15 test questions

Built from the content of the 30 files of round 1 (20 files with a text layer, 10 scan files). The standard answers were drafted by AI from the automatically extracted text of those same files, without outside knowledge. **A subject-matter officer must confirm them before they are used as the standard.**

Conventions:
- **[Trực tiếp]** (Direct): the instrument states it outright. **[Tổng hợp]** (Synthesis): several clauses or several instruments must be combined. **[Đủ dữ kiện?]** (Enough facts?): a conclusion is possible only when there are additional facts. **[Không đủ căn cứ]** (Insufficient basis): the 30 files do not contain the information, and the system must say so clearly.
- Page numbers of Word files (.doc, .docx) are the pages of the PDF that the app converted from Word. Page numbers of scan files are the pages of the scan. Page numbers of a text-layer PDF are the original pages.
- The OCR text contains errors (for example, the number of Circular 58/2026 was read as "2Ÿ /2026"). Answers rely on the number in the file name and on the portion of the content that could be read.
